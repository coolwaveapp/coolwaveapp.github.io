Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · For residents of Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and other US states with consumer health data laws

In short
  • Coolwave handles health information only on your iPhone, and only after you give consent in the app.
  • We don't receive it, sell it, share it or use it for advertising.
  • You can see, export, delete it or withdraw consent at any time in the app.

This policy explains how Coolwave, made by Maria Solans ("we"), handles consumer health data as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370, Connecticut's data privacy law and similar laws. It adds to our Privacy Policy.

Consumer health data Coolwave handles, and why

CategoryPurpose
Hot flashes and night sweats (time, intensity, triggers)Show your log, patterns and reports
Symptoms, mood, sleep and bleeding from daily check-insShow your patterns and include them in your report
Treatments, medication, doses and lab resultsTrack your treatment, send the reminders you set and show changes since you started
Menopause stage you chooseAdapt what the app shows you
Sleep from Apple Health (optional)Fill in check-ins you haven't completed

We use this data only to provide the features you ask for.

Sources

You, when you enter information in the app or its widgets, and Apple Health, if you allow it.

Where it's kept

Only on your iPhone. Coolwave has no servers that receive your health data, and we can't see it.

Sharing

We don't share consumer health data with any third party or affiliate, and we don't sell it. If you choose to save hot flashes to Apple Health, they're stored by iOS on your device. If you export a report and send it to someone, that's your choice and goes only to the people you pick.

Consent

Coolwave asks for your explicit consent before it keeps any health data, in a separate step when you first open the app. You can see when you gave it in Profile › Privacy.

Your rights and how to use them

  • Know and access: everything Coolwave holds is visible in the app. Profile › Export all my data gives you a full copy (CSV).
  • Delete: Profile › Delete all my data, or delete the app.
  • Withdraw consent: Profile › Withdraw consent. This deletes your data and stops further handling.
  • Help or appeal: write to coolwave@use.startmail.com. We'll answer within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and you may contact your state Attorney General.

We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

No geofencing

Coolwave doesn't use your location and doesn't set virtual boundaries around health care locations.

Changes

If we ever plan to handle consumer health data differently (for example, an optional cloud backup), we'll update this policy and ask for your consent first.

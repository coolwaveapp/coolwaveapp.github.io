Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

In short
  • Everything you log in Coolwave stays on your iPhone. We don't run servers that receive it.
  • There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We never sell, rent or share your health data.
  • You can export or delete everything from the app at any time.

Who we are

Coolwave is made by Maria Solans, an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to coolwave@use.startmail.com.

What Coolwave handles

To work, the app stores the information you choose to add:

  • Hot flashes and night sweats: time, intensity and possible triggers.
  • Daily check-ins: symptoms, triggers, sleep, mood and, if you track it, bleeding.
  • Treatments and medication, dose changes, reminder times and lab results.
  • Questions for your doctor, your stage and app settings.

This information is saved only on your iPhone, in Coolwave's private storage (shared with Coolwave's own widgets). It's protected by iOS Data Protection, and you can add a Face ID lock in Profile.

What we collect

Nothing. Coolwave doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn't use your data to profile you.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you allow it, Coolwave reads your sleep from Apple Health to fill in check-ins, and saves the hot flashes and night sweats you log, with their intensity. This is optional and you can change it at any time in the Health app or in Coolwave's Profile.

Health data is processed only on your device. It's never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties and never stored by us in iCloud or any other cloud.

Notifications and Face ID

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; no notification server is involved. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS: Coolwave only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Purchases

Coolwave Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment and manages your subscription; we never see your payment details. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information Apple may share with us

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics. They don't include the health information you log in Coolwave. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

When you share something

You decide when to share. If you export your doctor report (PDF) or your data (CSV) and send it by email, message or AirDrop, it goes only to the people you choose, through the app you choose.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your Coolwave data is included in that backup under your Apple Account. We can't access your backups.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it.
  • Profile › Delete all my data removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes it from your iPhone.
  • Profile › Withdraw consent deletes your data and returns the app to its first screen.
  • Hot flashes you saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or US state privacy and health data laws), you have rights to access, correct, delete and export your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because your data never leaves your iPhone, you can exercise all of them directly in the app. If you need help, write to coolwave@use.startmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

US residents: see also our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Children

Coolwave is meant for adults going through perimenopause and menopause. It isn't directed to children, and we don't knowingly handle children's data.

Not medical advice

Coolwave is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.

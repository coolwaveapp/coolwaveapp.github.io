Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to coolwave@use.startmail.com. We usually answer within 2 working days.

Logging

How do I log a hot flash?

Tap the big Hot flash button on Today. To set how strong it was, hold the button: it moves from mild to moderate to severe. Let go when it matches. Missed one? Tap "Log an earlier one".

How do I add the widget?
  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Coolwave.
  3. Choose the small or medium widget. The medium one has a button for each intensity.

You can also add Coolwave to Control Center, or say "Log a hot flash in Coolwave" to Siri. Lock Screen widgets open the app, so nothing gets logged by accident.

Can I undo or delete a hot flash?

Right after logging, tap Undo on the message at the bottom. Later, open Calendar, choose the day, tap "Show each hot flash" and tap the trash icon next to it.

Apple Health and reminders

Coolwave isn't saving to Apple Health

Open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps › Coolwave and turn on Hot Flashes and Night Sweats. In Coolwave, check Profile › Apple Health is on.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Settings › Notifications › Coolwave and turn on Allow Notifications. In Coolwave, check the times in Profile › Reminders. The evening reminder is skipped on days you've already checked in, and treatment reminders stop once you mark them as done.

Premium and subscriptions

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Coolwave, go to Profile and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Coolwave › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged. You keep Premium until the end of the period. You can also go there from Coolwave › Profile › Manage subscription.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose Coolwave.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Coolwave has no account and no servers that receive your data. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your Coolwave data moves with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, or with Quick Start when you set up the new one.

How do I export or delete my data?

Profile › Export all my data creates a CSV file. Profile › Delete all my data erases everything from the app; Withdraw consent does the same and starts the app from the beginning.

Medical questions

Coolwave is a personal tracker, not a medical device, and we can't give medical advice. Please talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your symptoms and treatment. If you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.
